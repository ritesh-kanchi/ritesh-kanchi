# Ritesh Kanchi
I'm a PhD student at [Harvard University](https://seas.harvard.edu/) in the [Intelligent Interactive Systems Group](https://iis.seas.harvard.edu/), advised by [Dr. Krzysztof Gajos](https://kgajos.seas.harvard.edu/). I broadly research Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), which lies at the intersection of computer science and design; specifically, I research accessibility, intelligent user interfaces, and computer science education.

I explore how people and computers understand user interfaces, and develop intelligent systems that bridge accessibility gaps and reduce cognitive barriers. My work involves modeling user behavior and preferences to develop systems that are personalized, context-aware, and adaptive, providing seamless support in everyday interactions. Additionally, my research in CS education focuses on accessible computing and supportive student learning environments.

Prior to Harvard, I completed my bachelor's degree in Computer Science at the [University of Washington](https://cs.uw.edu), working on [accessibility research](https://makeabilitylab.cs.washington.edu/), [introductory computer science education/ressearch](https://courses.cs.washington.edu/courses/cse14x/ta/alumni.php), and [advanced robotics](https://aruw.org).
